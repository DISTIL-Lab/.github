Welcome to Distributed \& Interactive SysTems Innovation Laboratory


DISTIL is a research laboratory at Luleå University of Technology (LTU), Campus Skellefteå, Sweden.

Our research focuses on distributed and interactive computing systems, with particular interests in cloud and edge computing, mobile and heterogeneous networks, Quality of Experience (QoE), mobile and pervasive computing, and intelligent interactive applications.

We investigate how computing, communication, and intelligent systems can work together to provide reliable, adaptive, and high-quality user experiences. Our research spans areas such as QoE modelling and prediction, cloud-based applications, mobile networking, context-aware systems, immersive and interactive technologies, and AI-driven systems.
